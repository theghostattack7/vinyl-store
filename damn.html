DAMN

Released April 14, 2017

Length: 54:54

DAMN. is the fourth studio album by American rapper Kendrick Lamar. It was released on April 14, 2017, through Top Dawg Entertainment, Aftermath Entertainment, and Interscope Records. The album contains fourteen tracks and has a direct, hard-hitting sound that contrasts with the sprawling jazz influences of his previous project. It quickly became one of the most talked-about releases of the year.

The title and the album's central question are about the struggle between wickedness and weakness. Lamar explores whether the problems in people's lives come from their own moral failures or from the pressures around them. Songs such as "PRIDE.," "LUST.," "LOVE.," and "FEAR." look at different sides of human behavior and show how emotions and temptations affect decisions.

A major theme is faith and fear of judgment. Throughout the album, Lamar speaks about guilt, pride, loyalty, fame, and mortality, often using religious and biblical references. His lyrics balance personal confession with broader commentary on American society, creating a project that feels both introspective and confrontational.

The album's structure includes a memorable framing device. The opening track "BLOOD." tells a short story that ends in a shocking moment, while the closing track "DUCKWORTH." reveals a surprising connection between Lamar's father and Top Dawg Entertainment founder Anthony Tiffith. The story shows how a small decision years earlier shaped the course of Lamar's life, and the album ends in a way that loops back to the beginning.

DAMN. features guest appearances from Rihanna on "LOYALTY.," U2 on "XXX.," and Zacari on "LOVE." These collaborations bring different musical styles into the project while keeping the focus on Lamar's writing. Rihanna's appearance in particular helped the album reach a wider mainstream audience.

Production was handled by a large group of producers, including Mike WiLL Made-It, Sounwave, DJ Dahi, Bēkon, Steve Lacy, The Alchemist, and 9th Wonder. The sound mixes heavy trap-influenced beats, soulful samples, and moody instrumentation. This variety allows the album to move between aggressive and reflective moments without losing its overall focus.

The album produced several major singles, including "HUMBLE.," "DNA.," "LOYALTY.," and "LOVE." "HUMBLE." became one of Lamar's biggest hits and was praised for its simple but powerful message about ego and authenticity. "DNA." stood out for its intense delivery and changes in rhythm, showing how strongly Lamar can control the energy of a song.

DAMN. debuted at number one on the US Billboard 200 with about 603,000 units in its first week, the biggest opening of his career at that point. It received strong reviews and won Best Rap Album at the Grammy Awards, with its songs earning additional awards. A special collector's edition later released the album with the track order reversed, giving listeners a new way to experience the story.

In 2018, DAMN. won the Pulitzer Prize for Music, making Lamar the first artist outside of classical and jazz to receive the award. The prize was a landmark moment for hip-hop and recognized the album's storytelling and cultural importance. Today DAMN. is seen as one of the defining albums of his career, combining massive popular appeal with serious artistic ambition.
